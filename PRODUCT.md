## Users

Business owners and teams of any size, anywhere, who lose time or money to repetitive manual work (calling hundreds of members, answering the same questions, retyping orders, chasing payments). Many are non-technical and run their business on WhatsApp and phone calls. The founding market is Hyderabad, India.

## Product Purpose

KNETRA is a done-for-you AI and digital studio. It finds where a business leaks money, then builds the service that plugs the leak. Success for the website = a visitor books a free consultation (form, WhatsApp or email).

## Positioning

Audit first, build only what pays back. KNETRA starts from the customer's own numbers, ranks leaks in money per month, and builds only what clearly earns more than it costs. Agents work in Telugu, Hindi and English.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services, in 3 groups:
- **AI agents:** AI chatbots, RAG chatbots (e.g. hospitals), AI calling agents (e.g. calling 500 members), WhatsApp bots, personalised multi-agent orchestration.
- **Automation:** workflow automations, custom builds from scratch on request.
- **Creative & web:** video editing, graphic design, websites.

**DeskBuddy:** optional add-on. Connects to the KNETRA services a customer pays for and answers their questions about them. Never presented as required.

Website copy must be available in English, Telugu and Hindi.

## Product Principles

1. Numbers first: every claim and project is framed in money or hours, never vague benefit.
2. Meet customers where they are: WhatsApp, calls, Excel and their own language.
3. Honest proof: show working demos, never invented results.
4. Small pilot before big build: lower the risk of saying yes.
5. Humans stay in the loop: bots hand over to staff, give no medical advice, and the customer controls conversation logs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
