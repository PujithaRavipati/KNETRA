# KNETRA site system

Scope: whole site (Home, 5 offer pages, 4 use-case pages, Pricing, About, Contact, Privacy). Mode: Persuade. Visitors arrive from Google search, phone and desktop, comparing providers. Action: Book a free consultation (Formspree form, WhatsApp, email). Proof: leak calculator, scripted demo chat + recorded calling-agent clip labelled "demo", sample leak report labelled "Illustrative". No testimonials or client numbers. Home focal moments in order: leak calculator, live demo, service overview. Languages: English, Telugu, Hindi. Build: code-led, Next.js + Three.js scroll.

User-pinned: glassmorphic beige theme (replaces the Nixie steel direction; Nixie concept dropped by user). Open: all prices, DeskBuddy coverage, domain.

## Direction contract

THESIS: KNETRA is the eye that sees hidden costs. A real glass lens travels the page on scroll; whatever passes under it reveals its rupee leak. Refuses the beige-template default of a static serif hero over a row of equal glass cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm beige and sand ground, frosted glass panels with crisp white edges and soft depth, one Three.js glass lens (physical transmission, refraction of the page behind it) as the only 3D object. One warm accent for live rupee values and the primary action; green only for "plugged". No gradient orbs, no icon tiles.

STORY: Visitor watches the lens reveal leaks in a typical business's day, sets their own numbers in the calculator, tries the demo chat, scans the services the lens has already "seen", and books a consultation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: headline "We find where your business leaks money. Then we plug it.", one-line sub, primary "Book a free consultation", secondary "Try the demo". Right: a frosted panel listing a business's daily work (missed calls, manual order entry, late payments); the 3D lens hovers over it and the row under the lens shows its ₹/month leak, labelled Illustrative.

FORM: User-pinned glassmorphic beige world (overrides roll; previous choice signals-instruments-nixie-laboratory-counter dropped). seed:c3c04d194bd6594e. Signature interaction: scroll drives the lens down the page; each section it crosses (calculator, demo, services) refracts and reveals its hidden number. Motion grammar: lens moves with eased scroll damping; reveals fade in under the lens edge; reduced motion parks the lens and shows all values.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
